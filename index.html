Process

How I work

A simple, collaborative workflow from first message to final file.

  1. 01

    Brief

    You share your footage, references and the vibe you're after. We agree on format, length and style.

  2. 02

    Edit & VFX

    I cut for rhythm and impact, then build effects, compositing and the colour grade.

  3. 03

    Review

    You watch a draft and send notes. I refine until it feels right.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Final export in the formats and aspect ratios you need, ready to post.
